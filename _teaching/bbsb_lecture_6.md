## Gene sets

|----------------|-------------|-------------|
| <img src="https://github.com/OlgaVT/OlgaVT.github.io/blob/master/images/BBSB-6_6.jpg" width="25000">  | Cellular functions can be represented as gene sets. **A gene set** is a list of genes that share a common biological function, pathway, or localization.||
| <img src="https://github.com/OlgaVT/OlgaVT.github.io/blob/master/images/BBSB-6_11.jpg" width="25000">  | Gene sets are defined based on **ontology**: a collection of terms, with their definitions and relationship.||
| <img src="https://github.com/OlgaVT/OlgaVT.github.io/blob/master/images/BBSB-6_6.jpg" width="25000">  The Gene Ontology (GO) is a major bioinformatics initiative that provides standardized framework to describe the functions of genes and gene products. <ul>GO privides three main ontologies:<li>Cellular compnent - a cellular localization of a gene/gene product</li><li>Molecular function - the gene or gene product activity</li><li>Biological process - a gene function</li></ul>||
