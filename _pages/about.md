About me
------
I am an assistant professor at [the Bioinformatics groups at the Vrije University Amsterdam](https://www.vubioinformatics.com/) and a guest researcher at [the Institute for Computational Systems Biology Institute University of Hamburg](cosy.bio). I study mechanisms of adverse outcomes (diseases and drug side effects) based on network biology and omics data analysis, with emphasis on the role of alternative splicing. In particular, I am interested how alternative splicing rewires interaction networks in a tissue- and condition-specific manner. I also develop tools for alternative splicing-aware data analysis and evaluate existing approaches.
